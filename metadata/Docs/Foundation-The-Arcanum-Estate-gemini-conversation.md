Describe the different aspirations in the game

**Gemini:**
In the game *Foundation*, "aspirations" are a gameplay feature that provides different long-term goals and challenges, offering a more structured and replayable experience beyond the standard "play your own way" mode. By choosing an aspiration, you are essentially selecting a unique path for your village, which comes with specific restrictions and objectives.

There are three main aspirations, each tied to one of the game's estates:

* **A Prosperous Priory (Clergy Estate):** This aspiration focuses on the clergy estate and requires you to build a thriving religious community. The goals typically involve constructing an impressive monastery and generating wealth through religious goods like herbs, wax, and wine, which are highly profitable for trade. You are restricted from building a castle, which means you cannot engage in the military aspects of the game.
* **A Mighty Stronghold (Kingdom Estate):** This aspiration centers on the kingdom estate and its military. Your main objective is to build a magnificent stronghold and establish a powerful army. The challenge here lies in managing the military's needs, as soldiers and visiting troops require a variety of goods and luxuries, which can be a drain on your resources. This path restricts you from building a monastery, preventing you from using the clergy-based trade and income sources.
* **A Prestigious Burg (Labor Estate):** This aspiration focuses on the labor estate and creating a prosperous, bustling civilian town. The goals are centered around population growth, achieving high-density housing, and satisfying the needs of your citizens, including commoners and high-tier residents. In this aspiration, you are restricted from building both a castle and a monastery, so you must rely on civilian production, trade, and taxes to succeed.

These aspirations are designed to guide your village's development, forcing you to specialize and make strategic decisions based on the specific constraints of the chosen path. They are a way to add depth and a unique challenge to the game.

The three estates in *Foundation*—the Labor, Clergy, and Kingdom—are the core of the game's progression and social system. Each estate represents a different societal pillar, and your relationship with them determines which parts of the tech tree you can unlock and which goods your villagers can produce and consume. ### 1. The Labor Estate

* **Theme:** The Labor Estate represents the common people—the workers, serfs, and artisans who form the backbone of your village. It embodies the themes of hard work, population growth, and trade. This estate is crucial for the very survival and expansion of your settlement.
* **How It Works:**

* **Workforce and Production:** All of your common workers, from farmers and foresters to stonecutters and bakers, belong to the Labor Estate. Their needs are your primary concern.
* **Royal Taxes:** The Labor Estate's main mechanic is the collection of Royal Taxes, which are a primary source of income.
* **Housing and Needs:** As your village grows, you must provide food, water, housing, and eventually luxury goods to keep your villagers happy and productive. Satisfying their needs increases their "prosperity."
* **Unlocks and Buildings:** The Labor Estate primarily unlocks buildings for production, resource gathering, and commerce.

* **Resource Production:** Stonecutter Camp, Lumber Camp, Forester Camp, Quarry.
* **Food & Water:** Well, Mill, Baker, Berry Gatherer, Farm.
* **Luxury & Goods:** Tailor, Tavern, Carpenter, Blacksmith, Jeweler.
* **Housing and Services:** Common housing (Village Core), Market, Granary, Storehouse.
* **Monuments:** You need Labor support to construct **Great Halls** and **Royal Manors** (for the King), and to build extensions for other monuments like churches and castles. These monuments are often related to administration, commerce, and civilian life.

### 2. The Clergy Estate

* **Theme:** The Clergy Estate represents the spiritual and intellectual life of your village. It embodies faith, devotion, and the pursuit of knowledge. The Clergy are not just a religious group; they are also scholars and caretakers of the sick.
* **How It Works:**

* **Faith and Piety:** Clergy members work in religious buildings and create an atmosphere of faith. Your relationship with the Clergy is measured by their "Clergy Splendor," which increases by building and decorating religious monuments like monasteries and chapels.
* **Unique Resources:** The Clergy can produce specialized goods that are highly valuable for trade, such as **Wine**, **Herbs**, and **Candles**.
* **Healing:** The Infirmary, a building tied to the Clergy, is where sick villagers can be treated, preventing them from dying.
* **Unlocks and Buildings:** The Clergy Estate unlocks everything related to religion, health, and some high-value trade goods.

* **Religious Buildings:** Chapel, Church, Abbey, Cloister, Infirmary.
* **Production:** Herb Garden, Scriptorium, Vineyard, Apiary (for honey and wax), Wine Press.
* **Monuments:** The Clergy are essential for constructing **Monasteries**. The Monastery is a huge, modular monument that can house various clergy-related functions and is a major source of income if you focus on a trade-based economy.

### 3. The Kingdom Estate

* **Theme:** The Kingdom Estate represents the military, nobility, and the authority of the King. It is concerned with defense, expansion, and the prestige of your settlement. Its themes are power, control, and military might.
* **How It Works:**

* **Royal Support:** Your standing with the King is measured by "Kingdom Splendor," which increases by constructing impressive military monuments and providing luxury goods for the nobility.
* **Soldiers:** The Kingdom is the source of your military units. When you unlock and build barracks, you can train soldiers who can then be sent on missions to gain resources and fame.
* **Military Needs:** Soldiers and visiting nobility have their own specific needs, often requiring luxury goods like wine, fine clothes, and jewelry, which can put a strain on your economy.
* **Unlocks and Buildings:** The Kingdom Estate provides access to military units and monuments, as well as high-tier goods and noble residences.

* **Military:** Barracks, Training Grounds, Archer Range, Knight's Hall.
* **Noble Housing & Needs:** You must unlock buildings to satisfy the needs of visiting nobles, which are tied to the Kingdom Estate.
* **Monuments:** The Kingdom Estate is required to construct a **Keep** and a **Castle**, which are massive, modular military monuments. A well-constructed castle can grant significant Kingdom Splendor and provide a powerful visual centerpiece for your village.
