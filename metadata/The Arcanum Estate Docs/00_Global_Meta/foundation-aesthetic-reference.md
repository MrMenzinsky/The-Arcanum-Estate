# Foundation Aesthetic Reference

Baseline reference for the vanilla Foundation art style — used to guide 3D modeling decisions and to seed AI image-generation prompts when producing 2D concept sketches.

The Arcanum Estate must **sit visibly inside** this aesthetic while adding its own geometric vocabulary (icosahedrons, domes, formalized nodes) sparingly enough that it still reads as the same game. See [[estate-identity]] for the design principle.

## Overall style

**Medieval Provincial / High Medieval Folk Art.** Grounded in historical Western European architecture, rendered clean and slightly stylized with a highly visible construction focus.

## Aesthetic elements

| Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Architectural style** | Medieval (High/Late). Half-timber framing, exposed stone foundations, heavy wood construction, steep roofs. Avoids polished gothic grandeur in favor of sturdy, functional structures. |
| **Materials** | Visible and simple. Unrefined timber, rough-cut field stone (grey/brown), lavender/purple wood tiles for roofing. Worn but not dirty. |
| **Color palette** | Earthy, subdued. Greys (stone), earthy browns (wood/thatch), lavender/purple (tile roofs), white/ochre plaster (infill panels). No bright synthetic colors. |
| **Structure** | Modular and organic. Piece-by-piece construction, asymmetrical additions, visible scaffolding, irregular organic shapes (especially for player-created monuments). |
| **Folk detail** | Modest ornamentation. Small rectangular windows (often unglazed), wooden shutters, modest carvings, simple-patterned half-timber framing. |

## Key architectural characteristics

1. **Exposed frame** — Structural bones (posts, beams, trusses) are always visible, giving a solid constructive appearance.
2. **Verticality** — Important structures achieve height via stepped, stacked modules and steep, pointed roofs (not single-pour towers).
3. **Clean lines** — Texture work is clean, keeping focus on geometry and modular assembly. **Critical for 3D modeling discipline.**

## Arcanum Estate departures

Where the Arcanum Estate consciously diverges from this baseline (do this deliberately, not by default):

- **Geometric forms** mixed in — icosahedrons, domes, formalized nodes. Most concentrated in the [[arcane-lyceum]]; used sparingly elsewhere.
- **Lavender/purple metal** for splendor — the estate's signature heraldry color. Distinct from vanilla yellow/silver/orange.
- **Sprawling courtyard layout** (the Aethercourt as a campus, not a single building) rather than the dense vertical layouts of vanilla monuments.

## AI prompt pre-description

For seeding image-generation prompts when producing 2D concept sketches:

> Art style is **stylized High Medieval Provincial (Western European)**. Buildings are **modular** with heavy, exposed **half-timber framing** over rough-cut **field stone** foundations.
>
> Key materials and colors are **unbleached oak/pine timber**, **grey/brown stone**, **white or ochre plaster infill**, and **deep red terracotta tile** or brown thatched roofing. **Avoid gothic arches** or overly refined elements. Structures must look **sturdy, functional, and clean** (not grungy). Detail should focus on **visible construction elements** and **steep, modular rooflines**.

When prompting for Arcanum Estate buildings specifically, append: *"with subtle geometric ornamentation (dome elements, icosahedron roof variants) on the most important structures."*
