# Druid Ranks — Decision Log

## Final state

The Druid path uses an ancient-wisdom progression. After a long detour through
power/essence/mastery framings, the design **reverted to the original
age-based titles** — reframed as spiritual connection to ancient wisdom rather
than literal lifespan.

| Tier | Rank | Lvl Range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | **Druid** | 1–5 | Entry-level specialist. |
| Tier 2 | **Elder Druid** | 6–10 | Growing connection to the ancient wisdom of nature. |
| Tier 3 | **Ancient Druid** | 11–15 | Deepest, most enduring power of the natural world. |
| Tier 4 | **Earthparent** | 16+ (hidden) | Hidden, quest-unlocked apex. Direct connection to the source of life. Gender-inclusive replacement for Earthmother/Earthfather. |

## Iteration history

**This was the most chaotic rank discussion in the entire conversation —
roughly ten distinct cycles before reverting to the starting point.** Anton's
own initial instinct (age-based) won. The lesson of this file: **simplicity
won.** Every reframe sounded clever in isolation but drifted further from the
thematic feel Anton was after.

The cycles, in order:

1. **L2866–2883 — Age-based (initial proposal):** Druid → Elder Druid → Ancient Druid → Earthparent. Anton's instinct. Gemini at L2880–2881 also floated **First Druid / Primal Druid** as T3 alternatives and **Primal Voice / Warden of Gaia** as T4 alternatives.
2. **L2887–2911 — Nature Mastery:** Druid → Wild Druid → Warden Druid → Primal Voice. Anton: "let's try some more options."
3. **L2914–2935 — Life & Authority:** Druid → Root Druid → Sovereign Druid → Gaia's Voice. Anton: "Not quite, let's continue."
4. **L2939–2971 — Force & Shaping vs Classic Hierarchy (two parallel options):**
   - Wildforce / World Shaper / The Source, **or**
   - Arch-Druid / Grand Druid / Primal Lord.
   Anton: "they still don't feel right. Let's try to have the word Druid in the titles."
5. **L2975–3004 — Class-Name Progression:** Druid → Primal Druid → Arch-Druid → Primal Lord / The Source. Anton rejects: Arch- is reserved for Mage's Tier 4; Primal Druid is hard to put on a scale.
6. **L3008–3033 — Wisdom & Foresight:** Druid → Seer Druid → Oracle Druid → The Source. Anton: "We might be onto something, but they still don't feel right. Especially the final rank. I'm still more inclined towards Earthmother/father or something with Gaia."
7. **L3037–3064 — Domain-Scale (locking Earthparent):** Druid → Grove Druid → World Druid → Earthparent. Anton: "Grove Druid feels ok, but world druid isn't connected to nature in the same way that grove indicates."
8. **L3068–3092 — Place → Essence:** Druid → Grove Druid → Telluric Druid / Primal Druid → Earthparent. Anton rejects: Telluric is too niche/academic, and Tier 3 shouldn't be on a world scale (no other path claims world dominion at Tier 3).
9. **L3096–3123 — Skill & Authority:** Druid → Master Druid → Prime Druid → Earthparent. Anton: "No unfortunately, it feels like we're drifting further away than the feeling I had with Druid - Elder Druid - Ancient Druid..."
10. **L3127–3147 — REVERT to age-based:** locked in Druid → Elder Druid → Ancient Druid → Earthparent, reframed as ancient-wisdom (not literal age).
11. **L3263–3268 — Final consolidated rank table** across all four paths confirms the reverted age-based progression.

## Abandoned options (consolidated)

Tier 2 attempts: Wild Druid, Root Druid, Wildforce, Arch-Druid, Primal Druid, Seer Druid, Grove Druid, Master Druid.

Tier 3 attempts: First Druid, Primal Druid (initial), Warden Druid, Sovereign Druid, World Shaper, Grand Druid, Arch-Druid, Oracle Druid, World Druid, Telluric Druid, Primal Druid (again), Prime Druid.

Tier 4 attempts: Primal Voice, Warden of Gaia, Gaia's Voice, The Source, Primal Lord.

Naming patterns rejected for cross-path reasons:
- **Arch-** anything: reserved for the Mage's Tier 4 (Archmage).
- **Supreme / Prime** anything in T4: reserved for the Sorcerer's Tier 4 (Supreme Sorcerer).
- "World" scope at T3: no other path claims the world as its dominion at intermediate tier.

## Design lesson

Anton's first instinct — the age-based naming — was correct. The middle nine
cycles taught the project something useful about its own taste: **the Druid
identity is rooted in deep time and ancient wisdom**, not in raw power,
mastery hierarchy, or domain scale. When in doubt, trust the first feel.
