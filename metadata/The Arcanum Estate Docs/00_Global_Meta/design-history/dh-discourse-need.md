# Discourse Need — Decision Log

## Final state

- **Discourse** is the custom Need that replaces the vanilla **Entertainment** need for Tier 2+ **Aetherians** (Arcanist and Loremaster statuses).
- **Description (locked):** *"Must attend advanced lectures and discussions to satisfy their intellectual curiosity."*
- **Fulfilled by:** Visiting the **Arcane Lyceum**, where **Loremasters** deliver the Lecture Service.
- Rationale for renaming: the vanilla "Entertainment" need is generic tavern leisure. Aetherians need a distinct, intellectually themed leisure activity so it is not confused with Commoner needs.

## Iteration history

- **Name shortlist round 1:** Contemplation, Enlightenment. Gemini recommended Contemplation as covering both intellectual (Mage/Aetherian) and meditative (Paladin/Druid) angles. Anton was unsure.
- **Description shortlist:** three options proposed; Anton picked option 2 verbatim.
- **Name shortlist round 2:** Erudition, Insight, Scholarly Pursuit. Erudition rejected (too obscure for players). Insight rejected (didn't feel right). Scholarly Pursuit kept as fallback.
- **Name shortlist round 3:** Meditation, Discourse, Intellectualism. **Discourse** selected — clear to players, directly matches "lectures and discussions" in the description.

## Abandoned options

- Entertainment (the vanilla name — replaced for Aetherians to avoid confusion).
- Contemplation, Enlightenment, Erudition, Insight, Scholarly Pursuit, Meditation, Intellectualism, Reflection, Instruction.
