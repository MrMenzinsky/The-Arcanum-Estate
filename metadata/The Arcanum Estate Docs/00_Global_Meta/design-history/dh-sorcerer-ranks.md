# Sorcerer Ranks — Decision Log

## Final state

The Sorcerer path uses an innate-power awakening progression. Titles are
gendered (Sorcerer / Sorceress) and steer away from the Mage's academic
hierarchy.

| Tier | Rank | Lvl Range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | **Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 1–5 | Entry-level specialist. |
| Tier 2 | **Awakened Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 6–10 | Innate, blood-based magic has fully come online. |
| Tier 3 | **Prime Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 11–15 | First in excellence — the best of the field. |
| Tier 4 | **Supreme Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 16+ (hidden) | Hidden, quest-unlocked apex. Classic high-fantasy ultimate. |

## Iteration history

Smooth path with two refinements after the initial proposal.

- L3152–3165 — initial proposal: Sorcerer / High Sorcerer / Master Sorcerer / Supreme Sorcerer. Gemini flags concern that Supreme was previously discussed as reused.
- L3169–3181 — Anton asks where Supreme was used elsewhere. Gemini confirms it was **never used** — Mage=Archmage, Paladin=Hallow Lord, Druid=Earthparent — so Supreme is genuinely unique to Sorcerer.
- L3206–3208 — Anton flags that **High** and **Master** overlap too closely with the Mage's academic hierarchy. Asks about word-order for the apex: "Supreme Sorcerer" vs "Sorcerer Supreme".
- L3210–3243 — Gemini offers **True Sorcerer** (T2) and **Prime Sorcerer** (T3). Recommends standard adjective-first order (Supreme Sorcerer) for clarity over the stylized post-positive (Sorcerer Supreme).
- L3247–3268 — Anton accepts Prime Sorcerer, rejects True Sorcerer, proposes **Awakened Sorcerer** instead. Gemini agrees it's a perfect thematic fit (blood-magic awakening). Locked.
- L3263–3268 — final consolidated rank table across all four paths confirms Sorcerer → Awakened → Prime → Supreme.

## Abandoned options

- **High Sorcerer** (T2) — too close to Mage's High Mage.
- **Master Sorcerer** (T3) — generic; also overlapped with Mage's earlier Master proposal.
- **Grand Sorcerer** (T3) — would have aligned with Mage's "High → Grand" academic ladder, which is exactly what Anton wanted to avoid.
- **True Sorcerer** (T2) — proposed by Gemini, didn't feel right to Anton.
- **Magus / Arcane Apex** — early Tier 4 suggestions, dropped once Supreme was confirmed available.
- **Sorcerer Supreme** — post-positive word order; rejected for being too stylized / too obviously borrowed from popular media.

## Design notes

- Gendered titles are intentional. Sorcerer and Sorceress are both displayed.
- The "Awakened → Prime → Supreme" ladder reflects the Sorcerer's identity as **innate / inherited** power, contrasting with the Mage's **studied / academic** identity.
