# Sacred Grove — Decision Log

## Final state
**The Sacred Grove** is the Tier 3 Druid specialization monument, housing **Elder Druid** → **Earthparent** progression. The architectural vision is *huge customizable trees and tree trunks* — the buildings are intertwined with (or *are*) the trees themselves (L3719).

**Originally a sub-building**, Sacred Grove was promoted to monument status at L3843-3850 after the standard naming process stalled.

## Iteration history
- **Earlier in the document (L935-970)** — Sacred Grove first appeared as the Druid **work sub-building** name, sitting underneath whatever the Druid monument would be called.
- **L3710-3712** — Opening monument shortlist: `The Primal Circle`, `The Earthheart Grove`, `The Verdant Shrine`. All rejected; user revealed the tree-architecture vision and that Sacred Grove was already the planned sub-building name.
- **L3726-3736** — Round 2 (height + ancient life + tree): `The Heartwood Spire`, `The Evertree Vault`, `The Canopy Court`. User liked the words **Heartwood** and **Evertree** but rejected all three structural nouns.
- **L3748-3760** — Round 3 (combining Evertree/Heartwood with sanctum/threshold/hearth): `The Evertree Sanctum`, `The Heartwood Threshold`, `The Evertree Hearth`. All rejected; user asked Gemini to *"tone down the enthusiasm"* and provide a plain list of nature place-words.
- **L3771-3781** — Reference list of place-words (Grove, Thicket, Glade, Hollow, Hearth, Threshold, Sanctuary, etc.).
- **L3794-3804** — Aside: clarified what `Hollow` means as a place-noun.
- **L3828-3839** — Round 4 (ten options): Evertree Cathedral, Heartwood Senatus, Evertree Archives, Heartwood Tribunal, Evertree Grotto, Heartwood Cloister, Evertree Dominion, Heartwood Apex, Evertree Hall, Heartwood College. All rejected.
- **L3843-3850** — **Pivot:** user proposed promoting `Sacred Grove` itself from sub-building to monument name, reserving `Evertree` and `Heartwood` for a future high-tier tree-building inside the monument. Locked.

## Abandoned options
- **Reserved for the future tree sub-building (NOT abandoned, just held):** `Evertree`, `Heartwood` — these words are kept in reserve as backup monument names and as the leading candidates for the signature interior tree-building.
- **Fully rejected:** Primal Circle, Earthheart Grove, Verdant Shrine, Heartwood Spire, Evertree Vault, Canopy Court, Evertree Sanctum, Heartwood Threshold, Evertree Hearth, plus all ten Round-4 combinations.
