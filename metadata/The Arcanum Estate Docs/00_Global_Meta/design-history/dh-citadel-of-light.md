# Citadel of Light — Decision Log

## Final state
**The Citadel of Light** is the Tier 3 Paladin specialization monument, housing **Holy Paladin** → **Hallow Lord** progression. The name was chosen for its blend of **martial defense** (citadel = fortified military core within a city) and **holy radiance** (light).

Locked at L3697-3698.

## Iteration history
**Shortest iteration of the four specialist monuments.** The user already had a strong gut preference for `Citadel of Light` from the opening shortlist (L3608) and only ran one extra round to confirm nothing better existed.

- **L3605-3611** — Opening shortlist: `The Hallow Hall`, **`The Citadel of Light`**, `The Templar Court`. User picked `Citadel of Light` as closest fit and asked about castle vs. citadel terminology.
- **L3621-3632** — Definition pass: a **citadel** is a fortification *within* a city, designed as the last line of defense and command center — fitting the Paladin role as the Arcanum Estate's military wing.
- **L3658-3668** — Round 2 (defensive/divine variants): `The Bulwark of Faith`, `The Divine Aegis`, `The Vindicator Keep`. All rejected.
- **L3680-3690** — Round 3 (exalted/solemn-vow angle): `The Exalted Bastion`, `The Virtue Sanctum`, `The Divine Command`. All rejected.
- **L3694-3698** — User: *"They aren't good either. Let's just go with The Citadel of Light"*. Locked.

## Abandoned options
- Hallow Hall, Templar Court, Bulwark of Faith, Divine Aegis, Vindicator Keep, Exalted Bastion, Virtue Sanctum, Divine Command. No backup name reserved — user committed cleanly.
