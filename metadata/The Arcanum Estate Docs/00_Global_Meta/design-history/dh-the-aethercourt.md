# The Aethercourt — Naming Decision Log

## Final state
- Name: **The Aethercourt** (single word, not "Aether Court")
- Pattern match rationale: matches vanilla Manor / Monastery / Castle — a single collective noun that names the estate via its monument
- Tier: Tier-1 monument of the Arcanum Estate
- "Court" reading: a grand, open-air complex of buildings with a ceremonial courtyard — fits the modular, sprawling vision better than "hall" (single enclosed building) or "hold" (fortress)
- Single-word spelling locked for branding: reads as a proper noun (Winterfell-style) rather than a literal description ("a court of Aether")

## Iteration history
(chronological — line citations from the Gemini chat)

- **L330–345** — Anton frames the problem: Labor has Manor, Clergy has Monastery, Kingdom has Castle; Arcanum needs an equivalent collective noun. Gemini's first batch: Academy, Sanctum, Nexus, Spire.
- **L349–355** — Anton rejects all four (reasons in *Abandoned options* below) and asks for a different angle.
- **L356–369** — Gemini pivots from function/shape to magical concept: Aetherium, Confluence, Orrery.
- **L373–375** — Anton likes **Aetherium** most ("close to Sanctum but a bit more magical, though maybe too much so"); asks for more in that vein.
- **L377–392** — Gemini offers grounded middle-ground: Vault, Citadel, Locus. Anton rejects all (L394–397) and reserves **Sanctum** as a Paladin sub-building. Asks for an Aether-variant that feels like a *building* (Manor/Monastery/Castle solidity).
- **L399–414** — Gemini: Aetherhall, Aetherhold, The Caelum.
- **L418–419** — Anton: "Maybe. Give me more options."
- **L421–431** — Gemini: The Crucible, The Observatory, The Reliquary.
- **L434–436** — Anton narrows favorites to **Aetherium** and **Aetherhall**; asks for "place of study" variants (academy/college/university but fantasy).
- **L438–449** — Gemini: Scholarium, Collegium, Lyceum.
- **L453–454** — Anton returns to Aether- variants; asks for at least 5 more building-suffix options.
- **L456–467** — Gemini: Aetherfort, Aethergarth, Aetherclave, **Aethercourt**, Aetherhaven.
- **L471–472** — Anton's shortlist: Aetherium, Aetherhall, Aethercourt. Asks Gemini to compare against Manor/Monastery/Castle.
- **L474–499** — Gemini's comparison:
  - Aetherium → most fantastical/alien, breaks the grounded pattern.
  - Aetherhall → closest fit to existing convention; "hall" = grand gathering place.
  - Aethercourt → grand, sprawling, elegant compound; ceremonial courtyard at center.
- **L3389–3407** (much later, after specializations discussion began) — Anton pauses to lock spelling: **Aethercourt** vs **Aether Court**. Gemini recommends single-word for proper-noun feel (Winterfell, Starfall) over the descriptive two-word form. **Locked: Aethercourt.**

> Note: the chat above ends at L499 with Aetherium/Aetherhall/Aethercourt all still live. The selection of **Aethercourt** over Aetherhall and Aetherium happened off-log between L499 and L3389; by the time Anton asks the spelling question at L3389 the name is already in use. Aetherium was reassigned to a sub-building (see below).

## Abandoned options
Names considered and why they were dropped:

- **Academy** — "too close to 'normal' academies, doesn't give the magic feeling" (Anton, L350).
- **Sanctum** — "close to Monastery / religion" (Anton, L351). **Reserved as a Paladin-specific sub-building** (L396).
- **Nexus** — "doesn't inherently sound / feel / associate with magic" (Anton, L352).
- **Spire** — "too narrow and specific; not all specializations will prefer high towers" (Anton, L353).
- **Aetherium** — appealing but "maybe too much so" — too fantastical, breaks the grounded Manor/Monastery/Castle pattern (Anton L374; Gemini L489). **Reassigned as a sub-building name inside the Aethercourt.**
- **Confluence** — not picked up; less building-like.
- **Orrery** — too narrow (celestial only); not building-shaped enough.
- **Vault, Citadel, Locus** — "none of these feel right" (Anton, L395). Citadel too military, Vault too storage-coded, Locus too clinical.
- **Aetherhall** — finalist; lost to Aethercourt. "Hall" implied a single enclosed building, whereas the estate is a modular complex.
- **Aetherhold** — too fortress-coded; overlaps with Castle/Paladin militancy.
- **The Caelum** — drifts celestial-only; not an Aether- variant.
- **The Crucible, The Observatory, The Reliquary** — each too narrow in symbolism (transformation / celestial / storage) to cover all four magic specializations.
- **Scholarium, Collegium, Lyceum** — too purely scholarly; miss the magical / monumental tone.
- **Aetherfort** — fortress connotation duplicates Castle/Paladin.
- **Aethergarth** — obscure; "garth" reads as garden/yard, not monumental.
- **Aetherclave** — too secretive/conclave-coded; wrong for a public estate centerpiece.
- **Aetherhaven** — too soft/peaceful; lacks the grandeur of Manor/Monastery/Castle.
- **Aether Court** (two-word spelling) — rejected at L3402–3407 for being descriptive rather than a proper-noun place name.
