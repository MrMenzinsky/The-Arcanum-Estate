# Sub-Building Promotions and Renames — Decision Log

## Final state
Three Aethercourt sub-buildings were workshopped during the early Gemini brainstorm. Each one shifted role between then and the current design:

| Origin (sub-building) | Current role | Final note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sacred Grove** | Promoted to Tier-3 monument | [[dh-sacred-grove]] |
| **Sanctum** | Remains a sub-building; centerpiece of a Tier-3 monument | [[dh-citadel-of-light]] |
| **Aether Tower** | Name retired; replaced by a Tier-3 monument | [[dh-atheneum-tower]] |

## Iteration history

### Sacred Grove (lines 935-970)
Originally workshopped as the Druid main sub-building inside [[_the-aethercourt]]. Candidates considered: Grove, Enclave, Druid Circle, Lodge, Cauldron, Root-Hall, Druid Grove. **Sacred Grove** was chosen (line 965) for its spiritual-yet-natural tone, contrasting the Mage Tower's verticality and the Paladin Sanctum's formality.

Promoted later in the design to a standalone Tier-3 monument — see [[dh-sacred-grove]].

### Sanctum (lines 974-1024)
Workshopped as the Paladin main sub-building. Compared against Sanctuary, Hall of Champions, Aether Shrine, The Cloister, Vigil Hall, Tabernacle. **Sanctum** chosen for its sense of an elite, ritualistic, holy-warrior space — exclusive rather than public.

Retains its sub-building role, but is the architectural and lore centerpiece of the Tier-3 monument [[dh-citadel-of-light]].

### Aether Tower (lines 1055-1171)
The Mage main sub-building went through the longest naming pass. Rejected alternatives: Spire, Observatory, Pinnacle, Orrery, Pylon, Beacon, Aerie, Zenith, Bastion, Colossus, Stormspire, Arcane Tower, Wizened Spire, Nexus. **Aether Tower** chosen (line 1147) as the least-bad compromise — connected to the estate theme without using "Mage".

Subsequently abandoned entirely: the name was retired and the Mage path's architectural anchor became the Tier-3 monument [[dh-atheneum-tower]].

## Abandoned options
- **"Mage Tower"** — rejected as too generic next to *Sacred Grove* and *Sanctum* (line 1097).
- **"Arcane Tower"** — rejected as descriptive rather than evocative; redundant with the estate name (lines 1117-1131).
- **All single-word verticality names** (Spire, Zenith, Bastion, Colossus, etc.) — rejected for failing to match the reverence of the sibling names.
- **Aether Tower itself** — accepted as a working name, then retired when the path was reorganized around the [[dh-atheneum-tower]] monument.
