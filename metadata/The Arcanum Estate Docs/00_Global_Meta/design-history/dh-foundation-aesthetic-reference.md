# Foundation Game Aesthetic — Reference

## Final state

Gemini's description of the vanilla Foundation aesthetic. Used as the baseline reference for 3D modeling decisions and for AI art prompt generation when producing 2D sketches as modeling guides.

**Overall style:** *Medieval Provincial* / *High Medieval Folk Art*. Grounded in historical Western European architecture, rendered clean and slightly stylized with a highly visible construction focus.

### Aesthetic elements

- **Architectural style** — Medieval (High/Late). Half-timber framing, exposed stone foundations, heavy wood construction, steep roofs. Avoids polished gothic grandeur in favor of sturdy, functional structures.
- **Materials** — Visible & simple. Unrefined timber, rough-cut field stone (grey/brown), lavender/purple wood tiles for roofing. Worn but not dirty.
- **Color palette** — Earthy, subdued. Greys (stone), earthy browns (wood/thatch), lavender/purple (tile roofs), white/ochre plaster (infill panels). No bright synthetic colors.
- **Structure** — Modular and organic. Piece-by-piece construction, asymmetrical additions, visible scaffolding, irregular organic shapes (especially player-created monuments).
- **Folk detail** — Modest ornamentation. Small rectangular windows (often unglazed), wooden shutters, modest carvings, simple-patterned half-timber framing.

### Key architectural characteristics

1. **Exposed frame** — Structural bones (posts, beams, trusses) always visible, giving a solid constructive appearance.
2. **Verticality** — Important structures achieve height via stepped, stacked modules and steep, pointed roofs.
3. **Clean lines** — Texture work is clean, keeping focus on geometry and modular assembly. Crucial for 3D modeling discipline.

## Distilled AI-prompt pre-description

> Art style is **stylized High Medieval Provincial (Western European)**. Buildings are **modular** with heavy, exposed **half-timber framing** over rough-cut **field stone** foundations.
>
> Key materials and colors are **unbleached oak/pine timber**, **grey/brown stone**, **white or ochre plaster infill**, and **deep red terracotta tile** or brown thatched roofing. **Avoid gothic arches** or overly refined elements. Structures must look **sturdy, functional, and clean** (not grungy). Detail should focus on **visible construction elements** and **steep, modular rooflines**.
