# Aspirant + Arcanist Work Professions — Decision Log

## Final state

The core economic progression splits into **two** work professions across the two status tiers, reflecting the theme **theory then practice**:

| Tier | Status | Work Profession | Building | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lvl 1–5 | Aspirant | **Scholar** | Academy | Formulas |
| Lvl 6–10 | Arcanist | **Caster** | Aetherium (within the Aethercourt) | Arcane Lore |

- **Scholar** captures the Aspirant studying the theory of magic — prestigious, academic, non-clerical.
- **Caster** captures the Arcanist performing low-level practical magic — the basic act of channeling, distinct from full mastery (Mage, Druid, Paladin, Sorcerer).

## Iteration history

The chat started from a single shared job across both tiers, and gradually moved to a split pair:

1. **Researcher** (Anton's starting proposal, single shared title) — Gemini flagged as too vague.
2. **Scribe** (Gemini suggestion) — rejected: too close to the Clergy Estate's monk Scribes in the Scriptorium producing manuscripts; the Arcanum Estate needs a distinct identity.
3. **Theorist** — Gemini's first reframe toward scientific/intellectual study. Anton liked the theory/practice contrast but felt the single word didn't cover both tiers.
4. **Practitioner** — proposed as the practice-focused counterpart. Kept alongside Researcher as an option, but ultimately also rejected as too vague and disconnected from the Aetherium.
5. **Apprentice / Attuner** — first two-job split proposal. Rejected — didn't match the theory-vs-practice framing Anton wanted.
6. **Tutor / Experimenter** — alternative split. Also rejected.
7. **Lector / Caster** — Lector for theory-reading, Caster for low-level practical magic. Caster stuck.
8. **Scholar / Caster** — Anton proposed Scholar as a stronger, more prestigious Aspirant title. **Locked.**

The decision to split into two professions (rather than one shared title across Lvl 1–10) diverges from vanilla's Kingdom Estate model (Spearman across both tiers) but was endorsed as a strong design choice because it makes the Arcanist's higher tier feel distinctly more skilled.

## Abandoned options

- **Researcher** — too generic; failed to convey what the unit actually does (L1360).
- **Scribe** — collides with Clergy Estate's existing Scribe profession in the Scriptorium (L1378, L1381).
- **Archivist** — early Gemini alternative alongside Scribe/Theorist; implied advanced text organization but never picked up (L1369).
- **Theorist** (as shared title) — too advanced-sounding for an entry-level Aspirant (L1408, L1946).
- **Practitioner** (as shared title) — too vague; weak link to the Aetherium (L1419, L1946).
- **Artificer** — Gemini's hands-on/creation alternative to Practitioner; not adopted (L1436).
- **Apprentice / Attuner** — didn't capture theory-vs-practice (L1965-1966, L1980).
- **Tutor / Experimenter** — didn't feel right (L1972-1973, L1980).
- **Lector** — eclipsed by Scholar, which has more prestige and clarity (L1995, L2007).

## Adjacent decisions noted in the same passage

- **Academy** building name was confirmed during this thread (L1915, L1918). Alternatives offered but discarded: **The Lecture Hall** (too plain) and **The Scholarium** (too archaic) (L1907-1908).
