# Aether Envoy and Grand Council — Decision Log

## Final state

- **The Aether Envoy** is the Arcanum Estate's "bailiff equivalent" — the chief administrative role for **The Aethercourt** that handles external affairs, communication, and quest reception. Analogous to:
  - Labour / Manor House → **Bailiff**
  - Clergy / Monastery → **Emissary**
  - Kingdom / Castle → **Royal Courtier**
- The **Grand Council** is the centralized external quest authority. Vague enough to act as an umbrella for the Aethercourt AND each of the four planned specialization monuments. Each monument can have its own contact within the Council (e.g., one Council member visits for Aetherians, another for Mages, etc.) — analogous to hosting the abbess / seneschal / king.
- **The Arch-Seer** is reserved as a potential high-ranking Council member; not yet committed to use.

### Grand Council — candidate faction taxonomy (L4840-4844)

Before Anton locked in *Grand Council* as the umbrella, Gemini presented three faction-type buckets the Aether Envoy could communicate with. The Grand Council was the synthesis of bucket #1, but the other buckets remain useful as flavor sources for individual Council member identities, sub-factions, or alternate quest givers:

- **High Magic Authority** (chosen direction) — examples: *The Grand Council*, *The Conclave of Mages*, *The Arch-Seer*, *The High Coven*. Quest flavor: retrieving forbidden knowledge, managing magical crises.
- **Ancient Institutions** — examples: *The Royal Academy*, *Ancient University Senate*, *The Library of Alexandria* (or in-world equivalent). Quest flavor: research, discovery, archiving.
- **Wealth/Patronage** — examples: *The Scholarly Patron*, *Wealthy Dynasties*, *The Sponsoring Duchess*. Quest flavor: securing funding, undertaking high-cost projects.

## Iteration history

- First pass options: **The Loremaster**, **The Magister**, **The Archon**.
- After Anton clarified the role is external-affairs/diplomatic (not internal scholar), the assessment shifted:
  - Loremaster → too internal, librarian feel. Demoted.
  - Magister → strong.
  - Archon → felt too absolutist, internal ruler.
  - **The Provost** added — strong academic/administrative fit.
  - **The Aether Envoy** added — strongest fit; *Envoy* is a direct synonym for Emissary, *Aether* ties to estate identity.
- Anton picked Aether Envoy.

## Abandoned options

- The Loremaster (reserved as a job name elsewhere — see Lyceum/Discourse).
- The Magister.
- The Archon.
- The Provost.
