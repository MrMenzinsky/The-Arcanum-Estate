# Paladin Workplaces — Decision Log

## Final state

The **Citadel of Light** monument houses the Paladin specialization. Sub-building functions are split because of a hard mechanical constraint: a sub-building can only have ONE function.

- **The Sanctum** — Function: **Need Fulfillment**. Satisfies the Paladin's unique spiritual need (working name: *Holy Reflection*). Ensures Paladins regularly visit the Citadel to maintain morale.
- **The Divine Forge** — Function: **Production Workplace**. Houses the **Reliquary Smith** job `[tentative]` (Gemini consistently hedged this as "Reliquary Smith *or similar*" at L4302, L4305, L4334, L4365, L4394, L4522 — never explicitly locked by Anton), which produces **Divine Seals** (a Tier 3 holy component, also used as the Paladin promotion cost — locked at L4535).
- **Training/Military Workplace** — **OPEN TODO**. Name not yet decided. Intended to house the military training job (working name: *Field Marshal*) and integrate with future Kingdom Estate military mechanics. Conversation was deferred at L4869-4905 when Anton paused the Paladin track.

## Iteration history

- Gemini originally framed Tier 3 Paladin jobs under a two-word alliterative naming convention (cf. *Wind Weaver*, L4272) with two example focus areas — Defense/Combat (*Sword Speaker*, *Shield Shaper*) and Holy/Support (*Light Keeper*, *Prayer Caller*). Anton overrode the alliterative format: job names can be anything, and there should be at least two workplaces in the monument — one production, one military/training, possibly split further (L4284-4285).
- Gemini's first three-job proposal split the work across two sub-buildings: **Sanctum** (Reliquary Smith — production) + **Hall of Arms** (Field Marshal — training) + **Sanctum** (Vow Keeper — spiritual buff). Each job carried a fallback alternative — *Holy Armorer* (producing *Consecrated Plate*) opposite Reliquary Smith, and *Aether Guard* (garrison/aura buffer) opposite Field Marshal. None of this three-job structure was locked.
- Initial idea floated combined Need Fulfillment + Production inside the Sanctum, but the one-function-per-sub-building constraint (Anton clarified at L4372) forced the split.
- Sanctum locked as Need Fulfillment.
- Production workplace went through naming exploration: The Chantry, The Forge of Vows, The Consecration Chamber, then a deep dive into forge/foundry/smithy distinctions, then a 10-option Forge-themed shortlist (Consecration Forge, Hallowed Forge, Vow-Forge, Light-Wrought Forge, Virtue Forge, Covenant Forge, Aegis Forge, Templar Forge, Anointed Forge, Divine Forge). Anton picked **Divine Forge** for simplicity and clarity.

## Abandoned options

- Sleeping Quarters function for Sanctum — rejected as low-impact, already covered by generic Annex lodging.
- Combined Need + Workplace Sanctum — blocked by single-function constraint.
- Production names not chosen: Chantry, Forge of Vows, Consecration Chamber, Consecration Forge, Hallowed Forge, Vow-Forge, Light-Wrought Forge, Virtue Forge, Covenant Forge, Aegis Forge, Templar Forge, Anointed Forge, Hallow-Smithy, Altar of Seals, Relic Chamber, Anointing Hall, Covenant Foundry, Bastion of Seals, Virtue Workshop, Templar Craft, Oath-Stone Altar, Sacred Censer, Blessed Chamber.
- Training workplace candidates floated but not chosen: The Watch Post, The Commandery, The Marshal's Ward.
- Alternate Paladin job names floated but never locked: **Holy Armorer** (production fallback, would have produced *Consecrated Plate* for high-tier unit armor), **Aether Guard** (defense/garrison — stationary buff-aura role at the Citadel), **Vow Keeper** (spiritual support — would have provided an AOE morale buff). Two-word alliterative example placeholders from Gemini's first pass: *Sword Speaker*, *Shield Shaper*, *Light Keeper*, *Prayer Caller*.
- Alternate sub-building name **The Hall of Arms** was proposed by Gemini at L4335 as a potential dedicated training building inside the Citadel of Light — never picked, but worth keeping on the table when the training-workplace TODO is resolved.
