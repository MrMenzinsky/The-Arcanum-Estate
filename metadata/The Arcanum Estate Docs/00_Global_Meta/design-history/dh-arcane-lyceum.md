# Arcane Lyceum — Decision Log

## Final state
The **Arcane Lyceum** is a T3 sub-building inside [[_the-aethercourt]]. It is the workplace of the [[loremaster]], who fulfils the **Discourse** need for all specialized T3+ units (High Mage, Holy Paladin, Elder Druid, Awakened Sorcerer, and beyond).

> **Naming note:** the source Gemini chat (lines 4948-5003) calls the need *Arcane Instruction* (with *Scholarly Contemplation* as a side option). This was the working name during brainstorm. The final, locked-in need is **Discourse** — shorter, less mechanical, and avoids redundantly stacking "Arcane" on top of every other Arcanum Estate label.

**Lyceum** (a classical school/assembly place, in the tradition of Aristotle) carries the academic prestige; **Arcane** ties it to the estate theme.

## Iteration history
Working candidates for the T3 lecture-space sub-building (lines 4970-4988):

- The Grand Lecture — too plain
- The Assembly Hall — too administrative
- **The Arcane Lyceum** — chosen
- The Aetherian Forum — debate-focused, not lecture-focused
- The Great Rotunda — shape-bound; locks architecture too early

## Architectural vision (locked)
Lines 4992-5009 record the intended modelling direction:

- **Domed lecture hall** inspired by the Roman Pantheon (oculus, coffered dome, monumental rotunda interior).
- **Open amphitheater** with a central podium and concentric stone benches (classical outdoor theater layout).
- **Modular roof parts** based on the icosahedron and other Platonic / geometric solids, selectable by the player.

The building should read as grand, open, and unmistakably academic — the antithesis of a cramped classroom.

## Abandoned options
None at architectural level — the Pantheon + amphitheater + icosahedron brief is locked. Earlier name candidates listed under *Iteration history* were rejected on tone only.
