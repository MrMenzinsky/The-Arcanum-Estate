# Atheneum Tower — Decision Log

## Final state
**The Atheneum Tower** is the Tier 3 Mage specialization monument, housing **High Mage** → **Archmage** progression and the elemental specialist jobs (Wind Weaver, Flame Forger, Stone Shaper, Mist Maker).

This monument **replaces the earlier `Aether Tower` sub-building** — the Aether Tower name was elevated/repurposed when the five-monument split made the Mages' container a full monument in its own right (see L3449 and [[dh-sub-building-renames]]).

Locked at L4115-4119.

## Iteration history
This was the **longest naming iteration** of any monument (200+ lines). Approximate flow:

- **L3417, L3449** — Opening proposal: `The Aether Spire`. User rejected, wanted to workshop alternatives once Mage jobs were defined.
- **L3468-3480** — Round 1: `The Elemental Nexus`, `The Conduit Hub`, `The School of Forces`. All rejected.
- **L3492-3499** — Round 2: `The Arcane Crucible`, `The Elemancy Hall`. Both rejected.
- **L3509-3520** — Round 3 (ten options): Runic Foundry, Sky Forge, Force Chamber, Tempest Sanctum, Weave Hall, Shaper's Vault, Grand Conservatory, Ethereal Hub, Mana Conduit, Caster Citadel. All rejected.
- **L3525-3541** — Round 4 (ten more, constrained to *science + four elements + tower height*): Arcane Observatory, Zenith Station, Elementarium, Quadrant Tower, **Grand Atheneum**, Apex Study, Aether Zenith, Force Obelisk, Elemental Lab, Theory Spire. `Elementarium` and `Grand Atheneum` flagged as close-but-not-spot-on.
- **L3553-3565** — Round 5 (variants on the two finalists): `Elemental Sanctum`, **`Arcane Atheneum`**, `Grand Axiom`. User favored `Arcane Atheneum`.
- **L3577-3585** — Round 6 (variants on Arcane Atheneum): `Aetheric Atheneum`, **`Arcane Collegium`**, `Esoteric Atheneum`. User kept **`Arcane Atheneum`** and **`Arcane Collegium`** as joint prime candidates.
- **L4095-4116** — Final pass: user asked to fold in the word **Tower**. Three finalists: `Arcane Atheneum`, `Arcane Tower`, **`Atheneum Tower`**. Locked on `Atheneum Tower`.

## Abandoned options
- **Backup names retained** (per user, in case of future change of mind): `The Arcane Atheneum`, `The Arcane Collegium`.
- **Rejected entirely:** Aether Spire, Elemental Nexus, Conduit Hub, School of Forces, Arcane Crucible, Elemancy Hall, plus all twenty options across rounds 3-4 and the Round 5/6 variants not kept as backup.
