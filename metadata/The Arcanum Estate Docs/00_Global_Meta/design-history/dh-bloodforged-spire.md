# Bloodforged Spire — Decision Log

## Final state
**The Bloodforged Spire** is the Tier 3 Sorcerer specialization monument, housing **Awakened Sorcerer** → **Supreme Sorcerer** progression. The name implies the building itself was forged/consecrated by the Sorcerer's inherited bloodline power — high-status, towering, and lineage-bound without locking to a specific color.

Locked at L4072-4078. **Backup name retained:** `The Binding Spire` (per user, in case of change of mind).

## Iteration history
- **L3870-3875** — Opening pair: `The Innate Nexus` vs **`The Crimson Spire`**. User liked the flair of `Crimson Spire` and floated `Ritual Grounds` as a possible monument or sub-building name.
- **L3886-3892** — Gemini's feedback: `Ritual Grounds` is too generic for a monument but excellent as a sub-building name. Recommended locking `Crimson Spire`.
- **L3911** — **User objection:** `Crimson` locks the monument to a specific color, which is too restrictive.
- **L3920-3930** — Round 2 (color-neutral, retaining flair): `The Sanguine Spire`, `The Lineage Tower`, `The Primeval Spire`. All rejected.
- **L3942-3954** — Round 3 (origin + status angle): `The Source Tower`, `The Lineage Apex`, `The Arcane Genesis`. All rejected.
- **L3966-3977** — Round 4 (ten Spire-prefixed options): Origin, Apex, Genesis, Lineage, Prime, Arcana, Unbound, Vein, Source, Inheritor Spire. All rejected.
- **L3985-4022 — KEY REFRAME.** User: *"Give me a list of traits for a sorcerer that uses rituals and summons familiars."* Identity shifts from raw innate power to **controlled power channeled through formal ceremony and contracts** — a ritualist/summoner who binds familiars. Gemini regenerated traits (ritualistic, binding, sealing, summoner, master of contracts, vessel) and proposed `The Binding Spire`, `The Ritual Conclave`, `The Evocation Spire`. User flagged **`The Binding Spire`** as having potential.
- **L4034-4043** — Round 5 (binding/covenant angle): `The Covenant Spire`, `The Evocation Spire`, plus retained `The Binding Spire`. User asked for one more pass.
- **L4055-4064** — Round 6 (ten final, lineage/vessel angle): Origin Pinnacle, Lineage Seat, Vessel Citadel, **Bloodforged Tower**, Inherited Apex, Arcane Font, Prime Conclave, Nexus Obelisk, Primal Well, Unbroken Chain.
- **L4072-4078** — User picked **`The Bloodforged Spire`** (Gemini's `Bloodforged Tower` candidate adapted to retain `Spire`). Locked. `The Binding Spire` reserved as backup.

## Abandoned options
- **Backup name retained:** `The Binding Spire` (preferred alternative per user note at L4073).
- **Fully rejected:** Innate Nexus, Crimson Spire (color-locked), Sanguine Spire, Lineage Tower, Primeval Spire, Source Tower, Lineage Apex, Arcane Genesis, plus the ten Spire-options of Round 4, Covenant Spire, Evocation Spire, Ritual Conclave, and the remaining nine Round-6 candidates.
- **Ritual Grounds** is *not abandoned* — explicitly reserved as the Sorcerer's Tier 3 work sub-building name inside the monument (L3886-3892).
