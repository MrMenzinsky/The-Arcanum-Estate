# Sanctum

**Sanctum** is the Paladin sub-building that originally was planned to sit inside the Aethercourt during early design. It now lives inside **[[_citadel-of-light|The Citadel of Light]]** (the Tier-3 Paladin monument) as its architectural and lore centerpiece. Function: **Need Fulfillment** (the Paladin's Holy Reflection need).

For full context, see [[military-workplaces]].
