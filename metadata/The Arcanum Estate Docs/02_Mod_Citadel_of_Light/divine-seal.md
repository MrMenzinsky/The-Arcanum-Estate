# Divine Seal

**Divine Seal** is the Tier-3 holy resource produced at the **Divine Forge** sub-building inside [[_citadel-of-light|The Citadel of Light]]. Also serves as the promotion cost for the Paladin → Holy Paladin → Divine Paladin progression.
