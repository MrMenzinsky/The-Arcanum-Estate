# The Citadel of Light

> **Out of scope for current development.** This monument is part of the Paladin expansion mod; not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships.

## What this is

The **Tier-3 Paladin specialization monument**. The Citadel of Light houses the Paladin progression from Holy Paladin onward and contains the Paladin-specific sub-buildings ([[military-workplaces]]). It's one of four expansion-mod monuments that branch off the core [[the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]] — see [[monument-architecture]].

## Name rationale

"Citadel of Light" balances **martial defense** (a citadel is a fortified core within a city — the last line of defense and command center) with **holy radiance** (light). The blend captures the Paladin's dual identity as militant *and* sacred.

This was the shortest naming iteration of the four expansion monuments — Anton had a strong gut preference from the opening shortlist and only ran one confirmation round.
