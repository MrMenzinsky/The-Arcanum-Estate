# Sorcerer — Specialization Design

> **Out of scope for current development.** This expansion mod is documented but not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships. See [[master-to-do]].

## Theme

The Sorcerer specialization is the **innate / inherited power** branch of the Arcanum Estate — magic that comes from bloodline, lineage, and intuition rather than from study or faith. Where the Mage represents *studied / academic* magic, the Sorcerer represents *awakened / channeled* magic.

The Sorcerer's identity went through a key reframe during design: from raw innate power to **controlled power channeled through formal ceremony and contracts** — a ritualist and summoner who binds familiars. This shapes the monument and sub-buildings (ritual grounds, binding circles) more than it shapes the rank ladder, which retained its "awakening" arc.

## Status progression

| Tier | Rank | Job levels | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | **Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 1–5 | Entry-level specialist |
| T2 | **Awakened Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 6–10 | Innate, blood-based magic has fully come online |
| T3 | **Prime Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 11–15 | First in excellence — the best of the field |
| T4 | **Supreme Sorcerer / Sorceress** | 16+ (hidden) | Hidden quest-unlocked apex; classic high-fantasy ultimate |

**Gendered titles are intentional** — Sorcerer and Sorceress both display in-game. This contrasts with the gender-neutral choices made for the Druid's Earthparent and the core mod's Loremaster.

- Additional sub-buildings `[TBD]`

**Notable design choice:** the Sorcerer is the **only specialization with no Aethercourt sub-building**. Every name proposed for one was rejected (Vortex, Crucible, Chasm, Forge, Crypt, Catacomb, Atelier, Menagerie, Conduit, Arena, Kiln, Foundry, Cistern, Barrow). The Sorcerer's architectural home is exclusively the Bloodforged Spire monument. This asymmetry is intentional — it reinforces that the Sorcerer is the chaotic, unstructured counterpart to the Mage's ordered scholarship. See [[abandoned-ideas#Structural|abandoned ideas]].

## Naming notes

The Sorcerer rank progression locked relatively cleanly — only two refinements after the initial proposal. **High Sorcerer** and **Master Sorcerer** were rejected for overlapping too closely with the Mage's academic hierarchy. **Grand Sorcerer** was avoided for the same reason. **True Sorcerer** was proposed for T2 but Anton preferred **Awakened Sorcerer** — better thematic fit with the "blood magic comes online" framing.

**Sorcerer Supreme** (post-positive word order, popular-media stylized) was rejected for standard "Supreme Sorcerer" adjective-first form. The Supreme name is unique to this path — no other specialization uses it.

See [[dh-sorcerer-ranks]] for the full progression iteration log.
