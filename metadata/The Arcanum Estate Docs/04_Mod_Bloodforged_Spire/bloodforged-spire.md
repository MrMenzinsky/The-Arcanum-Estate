# The Bloodforged Spire

> **Out of scope for current development.** This monument is part of the Sorcerer expansion mod; not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships.

## What this is

The **Tier-3 Sorcerer specialization monument**. Houses the Awakened Sorcerer → Supreme Sorcerer progression and the Sorcerer-specific sub-buildings. One of four expansion-mod monuments branching off the [[the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]] — see [[monument-architecture]].

**The Bloodforged Spire is the Sorcerer's only architectural home** — there is no Sorcerer sub-building inside the Aethercourt (by design). See [[abandoned-ideas#Structural|the rationale]].

## Name rationale

"Bloodforged Spire" implies the building itself was forged or consecrated by the Sorcerer's inherited bloodline power. The name is high-status, towering, and lineage-bound *without locking to a specific color* — this color-neutrality was a key constraint Anton enforced after rejecting "Crimson Spire" for being too restrictive.

**Backup name retained:** **The Binding Spire** (Anton kept this on standby in case of future change of mind).

## Naming iteration

This was the most thematically complex of the four expansion-monument namings. Six rounds before locking:

- **Opening**: Innate Nexus, Crimson Spire — Crimson rejected for locking to a specific color
- **Round 2**: Sanguine Spire, Lineage Tower, Primeval Spire — all rejected
- **Round 3**: Source Tower, Lineage Apex, Arcane Genesis — all rejected
- **Round 4** (ten Spire-prefixed): Origin, Apex, Genesis, Lineage, Prime, Arcana, Unbound, Vein, Source, Inheritor — all rejected
- **Key reframe**: Anton requested traits for "a sorcerer that uses rituals and summons familiars" — identity shifted from raw innate power to **controlled power channeled through ceremony and contracts**. New traits: ritualistic, binding, sealing, summoner, master of contracts, vessel. This reframe shaped the final naming.
- **Round 5**: Binding Spire (kept on table), Covenant Spire, Evocation Spire, Ritual Conclave — Binding Spire flagged as promising
- **Round 6** (ten final): Origin Pinnacle, Lineage Seat, Vessel Citadel, Bloodforged Tower, Inherited Apex, Arcane Font, Prime Conclave, Nexus Obelisk, Primal Well, Unbroken Chain
- **Lock**: Bloodforged Tower → adapted to "Bloodforged Spire" to retain the Spire shape word

The key insight from this iteration: the Sorcerer's monument name had to encode **lineage / inherited-power / ritualistic** themes simultaneously, *without* being color-coded. "Bloodforged" carries lineage and ritual implication without forcing a red palette.
