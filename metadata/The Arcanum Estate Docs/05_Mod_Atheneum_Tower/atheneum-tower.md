# The Atheneum Tower

> **Out of scope for current development.** This monument is part of the Mage expansion mod; not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships.

## What this is

The **Tier-3 Mage specialization monument**. Houses the High Mage → Archmage progression and the four elemental work professions (Wind Weaver, Stone Shaper, Flame Forger, Mist Maker). One of four expansion-mod monuments branching off the [[the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]] — see [[monument-architecture]].

## Name rationale

"Atheneum Tower" combines **Atheneum** (a temple to Athena, traditionally repurposed as a name for institutions of learning and culture) with **Tower** (the Mage's vertical architectural signature). The combination conveys scholarly grandeur with elemental height.

**Backup names retained** (in case of future change of mind): **The Arcane Atheneum**, **The Arcane Collegium**.

## Historical name evolution

This monument **replaces an earlier "Aether Tower" sub-building** that was originally proposed inside the Aethercourt. When the [[monument-architecture|five-monument split]] was decided, the Mage's container was promoted to a full monument, but "Aether Tower" was deemed insufficient for monument scale. The Aether Tower name was retired entirely. See [[abandoned-ideas#Naming renames (structural)|the rename log]].

## Naming iteration

**Longest naming iteration of any monument** — six rounds across ~200 lines of chat:

- **Opening**: The Aether Spire — rejected, wanted to workshop alternatives
- **Round 1**: The Elemental Nexus, The Conduit Hub, The School of Forces — all rejected
- **Round 2**: The Arcane Crucible, The Elemancy Hall — rejected
- **Round 3** (ten options): Runic Foundry, Sky Forge, Force Chamber, Tempest Sanctum, Weave Hall, Shaper's Vault, Grand Conservatory, Ethereal Hub, Mana Conduit, Caster Citadel — all rejected
- **Round 4** (constrained to science + four elements + tower height): Arcane Observatory, Zenith Station, **Elementarium**, Quadrant Tower, **Grand Atheneum**, Apex Study, Aether Zenith, Force Obelisk, Elemental Lab, Theory Spire — Elementarium and Grand Atheneum flagged as close-but-not-yet
- **Round 5** (variants on finalists): Elemental Sanctum, **Arcane Atheneum**, Grand Axiom — Arcane Atheneum favored
- **Round 6** (variants on Arcane Atheneum): Aetheric Atheneum, **Arcane Collegium**, Esoteric Atheneum — Arcane Atheneum and Arcane Collegium kept as joint prime candidates
- **Final pass**: fold in the word "Tower". Three finalists: Arcane Atheneum, Arcane Tower, **Atheneum Tower** — locked

The key insight from this long iteration: the Mage monument's name needed to convey **scholarship** (Atheneum) + **elemental mastery** (Tower verticality) simultaneously, without sounding either too literal or too fantastical.
