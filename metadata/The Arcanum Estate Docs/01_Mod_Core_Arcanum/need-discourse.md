# Discourse (Need)

## What this is

**Discourse** is a custom Need that **replaces the vanilla Entertainment need** for Aetherians at the **Arcanist** and **Loremaster** statuses, and for all specialist units once an expansion mod is enabled (Mage, Paladin, Druid, Sorcerer at every rank). It is the Arcanum Estate's flagship custom need — the mechanic that signals "this estate satisfies its leisure intellectually, not physically."

## In-game text

**Description (locked):** *"Must attend advanced lectures and discussions to satisfy their intellectual curiosity."*

## How it's fulfilled

Aetherians and specialists fulfill Discourse by attending lectures at the **[[arcane-lyceum|Arcane Lyceum]]** (a T3 sub-building of the [[_the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]]). Lectures are delivered by **Loremasters** — the T3 Aetherian status whose entire purpose is providing this Lecture Service. See [[aetherian-progression#The Loremaster — late addition, load-bearing|Loremaster overview]].

## Tier coverage

| Status / unit | Need? |
| --- | --- |
| Aspirant (T1 Aetherian) | No — uses vanilla Entertainment |
| Arcanist (T2 Aetherian) | **Yes — Discourse replaces Entertainment** |
| Loremaster (T3 Aetherian) | **Yes** |
| Mage / Paladin / Druid / Sorcerer (any rank) | **Yes** — from the moment a unit branches into a specialization, Discourse becomes a need |

The vanilla Clergy religious-service need is also bypassed by these high-tier units; Discourse is the **single unified need** that supplants both Entertainment and church/chapel service for the Aetherian + specialist population. See [[dh-loremaster-tier-3]] for the rationale.

## Why it exists

Two reasons:

1. **Theme** — high-tier Aetherians are scholars and magic-users. Drinking at a tavern doesn't fit. Attending lectures does.
2. **Structure** — Discourse is the mechanic that keeps the [[_the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]] mechanically relevant after specialists are trained. Without Discourse, the Aethercourt's late-game role evaporates. With it, every specialist building needs the Aethercourt to keep producing Loremasters.

This is the same load-bearing role described in the Loremaster decision: Discourse is one half of the two-part fix (Loremaster status + Discourse need) that wires the Aethercourt into the late game.

## Naming notes

"Discourse" was chosen over Contemplation, Enlightenment, Erudition (too obscure), Insight, Scholarly Pursuit, Meditation, Intellectualism, Reflection, and Instruction. The decisive criterion was clarity to players: "Discourse" directly mirrors the description's "lectures and discussions" — the word and the action match.
