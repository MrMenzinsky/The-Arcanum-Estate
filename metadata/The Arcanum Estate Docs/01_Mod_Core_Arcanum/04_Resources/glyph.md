# Glyph

**Glyph** is the Arcanum Estate's basic intellectual resource. Produced by Scholars (Aspirants) at the Academy; consumed by Casters (Arcanists) at the Aetherium to produce Arcane Lore. Stored at the Archive.

Also consumed at the [[aether-bindery|Aether Bindery]] (T4) as the primary input to [[enchanted-tome|Enchanted Tomes]] — deliberately chosen over Arcane Lore to keep the promotion economy unstrained. See [[dh-notebooklm-aether-bindery]].

For full context, see [[core-economy]].
