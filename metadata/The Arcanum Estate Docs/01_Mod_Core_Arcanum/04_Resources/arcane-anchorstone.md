# Arcane Anchorstone (empty)

**Arcane Anchorstone** is the Tier-3 base resource of the charged-container family — an empty magical container crafted at the [[artificer|Artificer]] from raw Aether Crystals + Iron.

The empty Anchorstone is **not used directly** as a building material. It must be **infused at a specialization monument** (or at a designated core-mod infusion workplace) into a *charged* variant before becoming useful. The charged variant then acts as a premium decoration material for floating / anti-gravity / luminous decorative parts.
