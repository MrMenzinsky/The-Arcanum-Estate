# Arcane Fire Anchorstone

**Arcane Fire Anchorstone** is the **Mage-path Fire** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused by a [[flame-forger|Flame Forger]] at the [[atheneum-tower|Atheneum Tower]] from an empty [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]].

Modifier `Fire` is the natural element name for the Flame Forger and is largely locked thematically. The specific infusion workplace within the Atheneum Tower is `[TBD]`.

For full context, see [[arcane-anchorstone]] and [[mage-design]].
