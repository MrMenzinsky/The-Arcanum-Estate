# Arcane Water Anchorstone

**Arcane Water Anchorstone** is the **Mage-path Water/Mist** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused by a [[mist-maker|Mist Maker]] at the [[atheneum-tower|Atheneum Tower]] from an empty [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]].

Modifier `Water` chosen as the canonical element. Alternative modifier `Mist` (from the Mist Maker job name) is also plausible; `[TBD]` whether Water or Mist wins. The specific infusion workplace within the Atheneum Tower is `[TBD]`.
