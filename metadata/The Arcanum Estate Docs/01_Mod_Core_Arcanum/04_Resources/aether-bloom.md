# Aether Bloom

**Aether Bloom** is a Tier-1 magical flora resource cultivated in the [[aether-bloom-garden|Aether Bloom Garden]] within the Aethercourt monument grounds. Consumed at the [[apothecary|Apothecary]] (T2) together with vanilla **Berries / Herbs** to brew [[aetherial-elixir|Aetherial Elixirs]].

Cultivation requires planter beds built mostly out of raw Aether Crystals — a deliberate early-game Aether Crystal sink before the Lapidary unlocks.

For full context, see [[core-economy]].
