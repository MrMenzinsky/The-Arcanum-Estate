# Arcane Pure Anchorstone

**Arcane Pure Anchorstone** is the core-estate's **unaligned / flavorless** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused by Aetherians (Aspirants / Arcanists / Loremasters) at the Aethercourt for use as a premium decoration material — the most prominent application is the **Floating Arcane Rune** T3-T5 Aethercourt decoration.

**Modifier (`Pure`) is one candidate.** Other strong candidates for the unaligned-magic modifier surfaced in the chat: `Origin`, `Source`, `Root`, `Genesis`, `Raw`, `Neutral`, `Unaligned`, `Clear`, `Stark`, `Essence`, `Ley`, `Formative`, `Resonant`, `Luminous`, `Kinetic`, `Astral`. NotebookLM's L1494-1496 recommendations were **Crystal Raw Core / Aetheric Unaligned Anchorstone** (flavorless emphasis) or **Crystal Origin Core** (root emphasis). Decision `[TBD]`.

**Infusion workplace** within the Aethercourt is `[TBD]` — the [[dh-notebooklm-artificer|Artificer pivot]] implies a designated workplace where Aetherians charge empty Anchorstones into the Pure variant, but the chat never named such a workplace for the core estate.

For full context, see [[arcane-anchorstone]] and [[core-economy]].
