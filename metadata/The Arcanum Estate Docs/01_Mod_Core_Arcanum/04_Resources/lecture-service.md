# Lecture Service / Discourse

**Lecture Service** is the service produced by Loremasters at the Arcane Lyceum. It is the **Discourse** need's fulfillment mechanism and the Arcanum Estate's replacement for the Clergy's religious-service need for T2+ specialists.
