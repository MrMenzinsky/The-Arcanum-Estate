# Arcane Radiant Anchorstone

**Arcane Radiant Anchorstone** is the **Paladin-path** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused at the [[citadel-of-light|Citadel of Light]] from an empty [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Surfaces concretely as the **Radiant Anchorstone** decoration at T5 Citadel of Light (per [[dh-notebooklm-expansion-monument-decorations]]) — "uniquely charged golden anchorstone hovering above the Citadel entrance to project an aura of safety."

**Modifier `Radiant` is one candidate.** Alternative path-thematic candidates surfaced as general Paladin themes: `Holy` and `Light`. Decision `[TBD]`.
