# Arcane Blood Anchorstone

**Arcane Blood Anchorstone** is the **Sorcerer-path** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused at the [[bloodforged-spire|Bloodforged Spire]] from an empty [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]].

**Modifier `Blood` is one strong candidate.** The Sorcerer T4 decoration "Floating Blood-Crystal" (per [[dh-notebooklm-expansion-monument-decorations]]) uses Blood as its modifier — though it uses Crystal not Anchorstone, which is part of the cross-mod naming inconsistency to resolve. Other Sorcerer-path modifier candidates from the chat: `Chaos`, `Surge`, `Warp`, `Sanguine`, `Soul`, `Vital`, `Prime` (collides with potential core `Prime`), `Essence` (collides with general flavorless candidates), `Source`, `Void`, `Nether`, `Abyss`.
