# Arcane Earth Anchorstone

**Arcane Earth Anchorstone** is the **Mage-path Earth** charged variant of the [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]]. Infused by a [[stone-shaper|Stone Shaper]] at the [[atheneum-tower|Atheneum Tower]] from an empty [[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstone]].

Modifier `Earth` chosen as the natural Stone Shaper element. Alternative modifier `Stone` was also implicit in the Stone Shaper job name; `[TBD]` whether Earth or Stone wins. The specific infusion workplace within the Atheneum Tower is `[TBD]`.

For full context, see [[arcane-anchorstone]] and [[mage-design]].
