# Aether Crystal

**Aether Crystal** is the Arcanum Estate's raw physical resource. Mined by Serfs at the Aether Crystal Mining Camp (an external building, not part of the Aethercourt). Stored at the Aether Hold; refined into Faceted Aether Crystal by the Lapidarist at the Lapidary.

**Early-game sink:** raw Aether Crystals are also consumed as the primary construction material for the [[aether-bloom-garden|Aether Bloom Garden]] planter beds (T1). This gives raw crystals a meaningful consumer before the Lapidary unlocks at T2.
