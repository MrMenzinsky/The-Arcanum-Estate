# Arcane Lore

**Arcane Lore** is the Arcanum Estate's prestige resource — the abstract output that drives splendor, parallel to the Kingdom's Royal Treasures or the Clergy's Piety. Produced by Casters (Arcanists) at the Aetherium. It is also the **promotion cost** that turns an Arcanist into a specialist or a Loremaster.

**Deliberately not used as an Enchanted Tome input.** During the [[dh-notebooklm-aether-bindery|Aether Bindery design]] the recipe was kept on Tier-1 Glyphs to prevent the promotion economy from competing with the trade economy. A playtest-driven swap to Arcane Lore remains an open balancing option — if it happens, the Aether Bindery would need a corresponding rename.

For full context, see [[core-economy]].
