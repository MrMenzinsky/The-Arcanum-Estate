# Faceted Aether Crystal

**Faceted Aether Crystal** is the Arcanum Estate's refined building material — the common construction input for the Aethercourt's sub-buildings (and likely the expansion monuments). Produced by the Lapidarist at the Lapidary by refining raw Aether Crystal.

Also consumed at the [[aether-bindery|Aether Bindery]] (T4) as the "lock" component in [[enchanted-tome|Enchanted Tomes]] — one Faceted Crystal seals the magical energy into each tome's heavy wooden cover. See [[dh-notebooklm-aether-bindery]].
