# Aether Bindery

**Aether Bindery** is the Tier-4 sub-building where Loremasters work as **Tomebinders** to produce **Enchanted Tomes**, the estate's high-value trade good. The name was chosen over Lore Bindery (would mislead players about Arcane Lore consumption), Arcane Bindery (UI collision with Arcane Lyceum), and Runic Bindery (too low/common for a Loremaster).

Recipe: **Glyphs + Planks + Faceted Aether Crystals**. The Faceted Aether Crystal "locks" the magical energy into the heavy wooden planks — the workplace description must tell the player this to make the lumber input feel intuitive.

The Aether Bindery is a **second viable workplace for Loremasters** alongside their primary role at the [[arcane-lyceum|Arcane Lyceum]], giving them an economic output once the village's Discourse need is satisfied.

If the recipe ever swaps Glyphs for Arcane Lore during playtest balancing, the workplace must be renamed accordingly — see [[dh-notebooklm-aether-bindery]].
