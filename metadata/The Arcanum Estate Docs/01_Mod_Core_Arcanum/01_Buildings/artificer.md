# Artificer

**Artificer** is a Tier-3 production sub-building where **Serfs / Commoners** (Labor Estate workforce, not Aetherians) craft empty magical containers ([[arcane-anchorstone|Arcane Anchorstones]]) from **raw Aether Crystals + Iron**. Mirrors the Arcane Weaver pattern of binding the Arcanum Estate's high-tier production to the broader village economy.

Recipe principle: iron-heavy, crystal-light (e.g. 3 Iron : 1 Raw Aether Crystal). Exact ratios `[TBD]`.

**Production-chain length:** 3 steps total — Mine (raw crystal) → Craft (Artificer empty container) → Infuse (at each T3 specialization monument's infusion workplace). Matches vanilla 3-step chains; deliberately stops short of a 4-step chain.

The Artificer's output (empty containers) is consumed by the T3 specialization monuments and the core estate, where designated workplaces *infuse* each empty container into a specific charged variant. The infused containers act as a **premium decoration building material** (floating runes, anti-gravity parts) — analogous to how vanilla uses Stained Glass or Sculptures. They are **not** required for basic monument construction.

**Originally** the Artificer was a luxury-goods workplace producing Aether Lamps (Faceted Crystals + Iron + Glass); that recipe was orphaned by the Tier-3 pivot. Aether Lamps' fate (drop / move workplace / become a higher-tier Artificer extension) is `[TBD]` — see [[master-to-do]].
