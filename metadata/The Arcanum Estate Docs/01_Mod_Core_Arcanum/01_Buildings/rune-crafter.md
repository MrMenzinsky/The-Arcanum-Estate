# Rune Crafter

**Rune Crafter** is a Tier-3 production sub-building where **Runic Talismans** are carved by combining **Glyphs** with vanilla **Polished Stone** (Labor Estate).

Polished Stone is highly thematic for rune-carving and forces a direct economic dependency on the Labor Estate's Stonecutters and Masons. The Talismans are a mid-tier buff item — either required by Paladins or sold at market stalls for a temporary efficiency buff to regular Labor villagers (use-case `[TBD]`).

Naming note: "Rune Crafter" is used as both the workplace name and (likely) the job name, mirroring vanilla `Mason Hut` + `Stonecutter` pattern. The chat doesn't explicitly separate them.
