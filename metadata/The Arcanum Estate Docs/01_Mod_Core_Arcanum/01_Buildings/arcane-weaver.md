# Arcane Weaver

**Arcane Weaver** is a Tier-2 production sub-building where **Serfs / Commoners** (Labor Estate workforce, not Aetherians) weave **Scholar's Vestments** by combining vanilla **Cloth** with **Aether Crystal Dust** (a Lapidary byproduct).

The Serf/Commoner workforce is a deliberate design choice — mirrors how the Kingdom Estate's weaponsmiths are normal Labor villagers, not estate-specific soldiers. Ties the Arcanum Estate into the wider village economy rather than letting it become insular.

For full context, see [[core-buildings]] and [[core-economy]].
