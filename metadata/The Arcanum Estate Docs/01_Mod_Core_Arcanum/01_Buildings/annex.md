# Annex

**Annex** is the Tier-1 lodging sub-building of the Aethercourt. Both Aspirants and Arcanists sleep here. Specialists (Mage / Paladin / Druid / Sorcerer) get their own lodging through their respective expansion mods.

In-game description (working): *"A place for your Aetherians to study and rest."* — modelled on the vanilla pattern of Manor House ("call home"), Monk Dorms ("pray and rest"), Barracks ("rest and heal").

This is the canonical place where the collective term **Aetherians** is introduced in the design.

For full context, see [[core-buildings]].
