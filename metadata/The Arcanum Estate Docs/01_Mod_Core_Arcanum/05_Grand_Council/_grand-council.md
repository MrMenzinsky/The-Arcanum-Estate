# Grand Council

**The Grand Council** is the umbrella external quest authority that the **Aether Envoy** liaises with on behalf of the Aethercourt. Vague by design — its members visit the Aethercourt and each of the four expansion monuments to deliver quests.

For full context, see [[aether-envoy]].
