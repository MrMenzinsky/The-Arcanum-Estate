# Arcanist

**Arcanist** is the Tier 2 Aetherian villager status, promoted from Aspirant. Arcanists work as **Casters** at the Aetherium. At job level 10 they can be promoted into a specialization (Mage, Paladin, Druid, Sorcerer) or into the universal **Loremaster** status.

For full context, see [[aetherian-progression]].
