# Arcane Lore

**Arcane Lore** is the Arcanum Estate's prestige resource — the abstract output that drives splendor, parallel to the Kingdom's Royal Treasures or the Clergy's Piety. Produced by Casters (Arcanists) at the Aetherium. It is also the **promotion cost** that turns an Arcanist into a specialist or a Loremaster.
