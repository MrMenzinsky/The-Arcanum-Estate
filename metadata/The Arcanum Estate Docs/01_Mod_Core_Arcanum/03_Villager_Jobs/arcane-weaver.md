# Arcane Weaver

**Arcane Weaver** is the work profession at the [[arcane-weaver|Arcane Weaver]] workplace. **Staffed by Serfs / Commoners** (Labor Estate workforce), not Aetherians — deliberate design choice mirroring how Kingdom Estate weaponsmiths are normal Labor villagers, not estate-specific soldiers.

Workplace and job share the same name (vanilla pattern: e.g. Tailor / Tailor Workshop, Mason / Mason Hut).

For full context, see [[aetherian-progression]] and [[core-economy]].
