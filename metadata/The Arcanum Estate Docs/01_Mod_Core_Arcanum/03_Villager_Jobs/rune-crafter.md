# Rune Crafter

**Rune Crafter** is the work profession at the [[rune-crafter|Rune Crafter]] workplace. Carves **Runic Talismans** from **Glyphs** + vanilla **Polished Stone**.

Workplace and job share the same name. Given the use of magical Glyphs as input, an Aetherian profession is the more natural reading.
