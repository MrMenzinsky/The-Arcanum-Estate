# Artificer

**Artificer** is the work profession at the [[artificer|Artificer]] workplace. **Staffed by Serfs / Commoners** (Labor Estate workforce), not Aetherians — deliberate choice to bind Arcanum high-tier production into the village's broader Labor economy, mirroring the [[arcane-weaver|Arcane Weaver]] pattern.

Workplace and job share the same name.

For full context, see [[aetherian-progression]] and [[core-economy]].
