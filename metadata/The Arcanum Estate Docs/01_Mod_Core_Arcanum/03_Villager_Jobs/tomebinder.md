# Tomebinder

**Tomebinder** is the work profession of a Loremaster assigned to the [[aether-bindery|Aether Bindery]]. Tomebinders produce **Enchanted Tomes** for high-value export trade.

Chosen over Tomecrafter (kept as runner-up alternative) and Illuminator (Anton's pushback: implies bringing light rather than book-making). The "binder" root preserves the medieval book-craft framing while avoiding mod-name collisions with the rejected "Bindery" workplace.

For full context, see [[aetherian-progression]] and [[core-economy]].
