# The Sacred Grove

> **Out of scope for current development.** This monument is part of the Druid expansion mod; not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships.

## What this is

The **Tier-3 Druid specialization monument**. Houses the Elder Druid → Earthparent progression and the Druid-specific sub-buildings. One of four expansion-mod monuments branching off the [[the-aethercourt|Aethercourt]] — see [[monument-architecture]].

## Name rationale

"Sacred Grove" combines **sacred** (spiritual reverence) with **grove** (a small wooded area, an intimate communion-with-nature place). The name is intentionally less grand than the other expansion monuments — Druids work *with* the land, not on top of it.

The name has an interesting history: **Sacred Grove was originally a sub-building name** during the early Aethercourt design (it was the proposed Druid sub-building inside the Aethercourt). When the monument-naming process stalled after four rounds of failed alternatives, Anton **promoted Sacred Grove from sub-building to monument name**. The sub-building slot now waits for a future signature tree-building. See [[abandoned-ideas#Naming renames (structural)|the rename log]].

## Architectural vision

The Sacred Grove's architecture is built around **huge customizable trees and tree trunks**. Buildings are intertwined with (or *are*) the trees themselves. The player customizes the trees, not just structures placed on the ground — this is the architectural distinguishing feature versus the other expansion monuments.

The reserved names **Evertree** and **Heartwood** are the leading candidates for the signature interior tree-building (`[TBD]`). They were extensively workshopped during monument naming and held back when Sacred Grove won the monument slot.

## Naming alternatives rejected

The naming process went through four rounds before pivoting to Sacred Grove:

- **Round 1**: The Primal Circle, The Earthheart Grove, The Verdant Shrine — all rejected
- **Round 2** (height + ancient life + tree): The Heartwood Spire, The Evertree Vault, The Canopy Court — rejected, but Heartwood and Evertree were earmarked
- **Round 3** (combining): The Evertree Sanctum, The Heartwood Threshold, The Evertree Hearth — rejected
- **Round 4** (10 options): Evertree Cathedral, Heartwood Senatus, Evertree Archives, Heartwood Tribunal, Evertree Grotto, Heartwood Cloister, Evertree Dominion, Heartwood Apex, Evertree Hall, Heartwood College — all rejected
- **Pivot**: promote Sacred Grove from sub-building to monument

The Evertree / Heartwood reservation is a hidden upside of the long iteration — those words now anchor the signature interior building.
