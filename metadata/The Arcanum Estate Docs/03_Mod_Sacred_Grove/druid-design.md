# Druid — Specialization Design

> **Out of scope for current development.** This expansion mod is documented but not implemented until the Core Arcanum mod ships. See [[master-to-do]].

## Theme

The Druid specialization is the **ancient-wisdom / nature-aligned** branch of the Arcanum Estate. Where Mages embody pure scholarship, Paladins embody militant faith, and Sorcerers embody untamed intuition, Druids embody **deep time** — patience, accumulated knowledge, and connection to the living world.

## Status progression

Aspirants who reach Arcanist may branch into the Druid path. The Druid rank ladder is built on **ancient-wisdom titles**, reframed from literal lifespan to spiritual connection with the ancient natural world.

| Tier | Rank | Job levels | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | **Druid** | 1–5 | Entry-level specialist |
| T2 | **Elder Druid** | 6–10 | Growing connection to ancient nature wisdom |
| T3 | **Ancient Druid** | 11–15 | Embodies the deepest, most enduring power of the natural world |
| T4 | **Earthparent** | 16+ (hidden) | Hidden quest-unlocked apex; direct connection to the source of life |

**Earthparent** is the gender-inclusive replacement for the earlier Earthmother / Earthfather concept. Like the Paladin's Hallow Lord and Sorcerer's Supreme Sorcerer, it sits behind a hidden quest.

## Naming lesson

This is the path with **the most chaotic naming history in the entire mod.** Roughly ten iteration cycles before reverting to Anton's original instinct (age-based). Branches that were tried and rejected:

- Nature mastery: Wild Druid, Warden Druid, Primal Voice
- Life & authority: Root Druid, Sovereign Druid, Gaia's Voice
- Force & shaping: Wildforce, World Shaper, The Source
- Classic hierarchy: Arch-Druid, Grand Druid, Primal Lord
- Wisdom & foresight: Seer Druid, Oracle Druid
- Domain-scale: Grove Druid, World Druid, Telluric Druid
- Skill & authority: Master Druid, Prime Druid

**Lesson recorded for future design work:** when in doubt, trust the first feel. The Druid identity is rooted in *deep time and ancient wisdom*, not in raw power, mastery hierarchy, or domain scale.

Cross-path constraints that fell out of this exploration:

- **Arch-*** is reserved for the Mage's hidden T4 (Archmage)
- **Supreme / Prime *** is reserved for the Sorcerer's hidden T4 (Supreme Sorcerer)
- "World" scope at T3 was rejected — no other path claims world-scale dominion at intermediate tier

See [[dh-druid-ranks]] for the full iteration log.
